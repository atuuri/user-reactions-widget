Reset your password

Updated 4 November 2026 · 3 min read

If you have forgotten your password or think someone else knows it, you can set a new one at any time. Resetting signs you out on every device except the one you are using.

Reset from the sign-in page

  1. Go to the sign-in page and choose Forgot password?
  2. Enter the email address you use for your account.
  3. Open the email we send you and select Reset password. The link is valid for 30 minutes.
  4. Choose a new password of at least 12 characters and save it.

Reset while signed in

Open Settings → Security, choose Change password, enter your current password and then the new one.

Didn't get the email?

Check your spam folder and make sure the address is spelled correctly. Emails can take a few minutes to arrive. If you signed up with Google or Apple, sign in with that provider instead; there is no separate password to reset.
